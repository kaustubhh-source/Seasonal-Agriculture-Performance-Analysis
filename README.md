# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis
**VOIS AICTE Internship Batch 1 (2026–2027)**

## Project Overview
This repository contains the end-to-end data analysis for the VOIS AICTE Internship Major Project. The project evaluates 4,000 agricultural farm records across 8 Indian states to study seasonal variations (Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid) in productivity, water efficiency, environmental hazards, and economic profitability.

## Repository Contents
- `Seasonal_Agriculture_Performance_Analysis.ipynb`: Complete Jupyter Notebook containing data cleaning, group-median imputation, seasonal aggregations, and Seaborn/Matplotlib visualizations.
- `seasonal_agriculture_performance_dataset.csv`: Dataset containing agricultural farm performance metrics across seasons.
- `VOIS_Major_Project_Submission.pptx`: Official 14-slide project presentation template populated with analytical insights and charts.
- `visualizations/`: Generated plots for yield vs. profit, irrigation economics, environmental distributions, and crop margins.

## Key Insights
1. **Seasonal Productivity:** Kharif records the highest yield (5.64 t/ha) and net profitability (₹1,78,915), whereas Zaid experiences net losses (-₹24,805) driven by extreme heat and water stress.
2. **Irrigation Efficiency:** Drip irrigation is the only system maintaining positive net returns across all three seasons.
3. **Pest Dynamics:** Warm, humid Kharif conditions drive pest risk to 54.5%, requiring timely protective measures.
4. **Crop Disparities:** Commercial cash crops (Sugarcane and Chilli) drive the bulk of farm returns, while staple food grains face thin margins under standard operational costs.
